# RTE AI Skills Transition Framework

A 90-day actionable roadmap for Release Train Engineers moving from "Process Police" to "AI-Augmented Value Stream Leaders."

## The "Survival Velocity" Progression
To avoid displacement by automated facilitation tools, you must explicitly shift your daily operations through three phases:

### Phase 1: Data Literacy (Days 1-30)
*Stop manually transcribing retro cards and gathering status updates.*
*   **Action:** Connect an enterprise AI assistant (e.g., SAFe CoPilot) to your team's project management tool.
*   **Action:** Practice writing deterministic prompts to automatically summarize retrospective notes and generate weekly sprint velocity trends.
*   **Goal:** Reclaim 5-10 hours a week previously lost to administrative toil.

### Phase 2: Dependency Sense-Making (Days 31-60)
*Stop manually shifting cards to find cross-team collisions.*
*   **Action:** Implement automated dependency mapping algorithms that scan code check-ins and feature descriptions.
*   **Action:** Run predictive risk analysis models (Monte Carlo simulations) using historical capacity data to forecast delivery friction weeks in advance.
*   **Goal:** Transition from reactive crisis control to proactive algorithmic forecasting.

### Phase 3: The "Empathy Moat" & Governance (Days 61-90)
*Start focusing purely on human-centric optimization.*
*   **Action:** Use the time saved by AI to aggressively target corporate political resistance and intense interpersonal team dysfunctions.
*   **Action:** Serve as the final Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) validator to catch AI hallucinations before they impact executive decision-making.
*   **Goal:** Tie your coaching initiatives directly to improved value stream flow and enterprise transformation, proving human judgment is the ultimate un-automatable asset.
